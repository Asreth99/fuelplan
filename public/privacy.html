What FuelPlan stores

  • Your account (sign-in method, email address and name as given by Apple or Google) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your saved cars (name, fuel, consumption, colour) in Cloud Firestore, in the EU.
  • No passwords. Routes and trip costs are not stored.

Deleting your data

Profile → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all your saved cars.
